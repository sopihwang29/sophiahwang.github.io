ClimbBot

A robot designed in SolidWorks that traverses inclines, picks up different objects, and places them on platforms to score points.

SolidWorksCADMechanical Design
📄 Read the project report

Overview

For Computer Aided Machine Design, I designed a robot in SolidWorks to compete in a points-based challenge. It had to drive up inclines, pick up different objects, and place them on different platforms to score.

What it had to do

  • Traverse inclines without losing traction or stability
  • Pick up different objects with a single mechanism
  • Place them on platforms at different locations to score points
